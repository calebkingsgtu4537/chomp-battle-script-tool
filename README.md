## What CHOMP Does

CHOMP is a browser-oriented VGC decision engine that works with Pokemon Showdown. Before a match starts, it reads the team preview and identifies four Pokemon to bring as well as two possible leads, giving you a structured way to evaluate the matchup.

The Gen 9 damage system checks every possible damage roll and provides both exact ranges and knockout probabilities. It can incorporate weather, terrain, screens, held items, abilities, speed changes, Intimidate, immunities, and setup states. Replay tools are also available for examining Pokemon Showdown battles and comparing team choices.

---

## Core Capabilities

- Selects four recommended Pokemon from the available preview.
- Identifies two suggested opening leads.
- Evaluates every possible Gen 9 damage roll.
- Shows the likelihood of achieving a knockout with the calculated damage.
- Accounts for weather, terrain, screens, items, abilities, and speed modifiers.
- Handles Intimidate, immunities, and setup-related battle conditions.
- Reviews Pokemon Showdown replays and compares team selections.
- Offers an independent calculator with 289 formes and 299 moves.
- Works with both classic and beta Pokemon Showdown clients.
